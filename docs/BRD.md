# Aarozi Healthcare - Business Requirements Document

## Business objective
Provide diagnostic centres and blood-bank-capable laboratories one operational system for patient registration, pathology, billing, referral accounting, reporting and blood-bank workflows.

## Business operating model
A centre can have multiple reception computers and staff doing the same kinds of work simultaneously. The system must treat the user identity and role as the authorization source, not the computer.

## Commercial model
- SaaS subscription
- annual offline license
- optional hybrid/cloud storage and backup
- configurable module entitlements
- tenant/branch/user/device limits per plan

## Primary business entities
Patients, tests, profiles, packages, orders, samples, results, reports, invoices, payments, referral partners, referral rules, commission ledger, donors, donations, blood units, cross-matches, analyzers, templates, licenses.

## Business outcomes
- faster reception workflow
- fewer transcription errors
- auditable result/report lifecycle
- automated billing/referral calculations
- traceable blood inventory
- offline continuity
- cloud reporting/backup when desired
