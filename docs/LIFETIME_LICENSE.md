- **Price:** ₹39,999 one-time + 18% statutory GST.

To ensure long-term commercial clarity and protect vendor support resources:

1. **Included Free Period (Year 1):**

   - Priority telephonic and remote desk troubleshooting support.
2. **Post Year 1 Operational Policy:**
   - The lab’s perpetual license **continues functioning offline forever**, regardless of whether they ever pay another rupee.
