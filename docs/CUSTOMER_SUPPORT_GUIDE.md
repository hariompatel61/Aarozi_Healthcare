# Aarozi Healthcare — Customer Onboarding, Support & Escalation Guide

---

## 1. Non-Technical Laboratory Onboarding Workflow (15 Minutes)

For non-technical laboratory owners and pathology technicians:

1. **Step 1: Download Setup Installer**
   - After online checkout, download `AaroziHealthcare-Setup-v1.0.0.exe` from your Customer Portal.
2. **Step 2: Run Setup on Master PC**
   - Double-click the installer. Click **Next &rarr; Install**.
   - The installer automatically configures the background service and Windows Firewall.
3. **Step 3: Import Commercial License**
   - Download `aarozi_license.lic` from your email or portal.
   - Open Aarozi Healthcare (desktop shortcut) &rarr; **Settings &rarr; System Licensing**.
   - Click **Import License** &rarr; Select your file.
4. **Step 4: Connect Workstations**
   - On reception and doctor computers, open Google Chrome and navigate to:
     `http://<MASTER-PC-IP>:8000`
   - Start registering patients and running analyzer tests!

## 2. Customer Support Channels & Remote Desk Consent

1. **Self-Service Portal:** Available 24/7 at `https://aarozi.health/customer/dashboard` for license re-downloads and hardware transfers.
2. **WhatsApp Technical Desk:** Official support number: `+91 98765 43210` (9:00 AM - 9:00 PM IST).
3. **Remote Desk Troubleshooting:**
   - Conducted strictly via AnyDesk or TeamViewer with explicit technician consent.
   - Technicians never request Windows passwords or inspect patient records.
